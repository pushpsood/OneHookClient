## 📄 Privacy Policy & Data Protection

OneHook is built with a "Privacy First" mindset.

### Key Protections
- **No Third-Party Sharing**: We NEVER sell user data or share it with data brokers.
- **End-to-End Encryption**: Messages are encrypted using TLS 1.3; we cannot read them.
- **Data at Rest**: All stored data is encrypted with AES-256.
- **Access Control**: Role-based access and MFA for all employees.

### Compliance Frameworks
- **GDPR**: Full compliance with rights to access, delete, and export data.
- **CCPA**: Rights for California residents to know and delete data.
- **COPPA**: Strict 18+ age requirement; no data collection from minors.

## ⚖️ Terms of Service

### Community Standards
- **Eligibility**: Must be 18 or older.
- **Prohibited**: Harassment, fraud, catfishing, illegal activity, and spam.
- **Enforcement**: Violations lead to warnings, temporary suspensions, or permanent bans.

### Safety
- **Identity Verification**: Multi-step verification to ensure authentic connections.
- **Reporting**: Users can report violations via the app or `security@onehook.club`.

## 📞 Contact & Support

We provide dedicated email channels for different types of inquiries:

- **support@onehook.club**: General help and bug reports (48h response).
- **privacy@onehook.club**: Data access and deletion requests (30d response).
- **security@onehook.club**: Vulnerability reporting (24h response for critical).
- **legal@onehook.club**: Formal legal inquiries and subpoenas.
- **hello@onehook.club**: Business and partnerships.
